Privacy Policy

My Child's MBTI (toddler temperament test) · Effective November 24, 2026. This policy applies to users outside the Republic of Korea; users in Korea are covered by the Korean policy.

Summary

  • Signing in is optional. If you don't sign in, your child's name, birth year and month (age), test answers and results are stored only on your phone (the app's storage).
  • If you sign in with email, your email address and the records above are stored on a server (Google Firebase, Seoul region) so your records carry over when you change phones. You can delete the account at any time in the app or on the account deletion page.
  • The test is free, and the app shows Google AdMob ads (rewarded, banner and app open). For ads, device information such as the advertising ID is sent to Google (section 4). Sharing goes only to the people you choose, through the app you choose.
  • Co-parent invite and result links contain your child's name, age and test answers. Send them only to family you trust.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Providing the test, reports and optional sync you ask for: performance of a contract (providing the service you request).
  • Anonymous peer statistics and service security: our legitimate interest in showing meaningful comparisons and improving the test.
  • Personalized ads: your consent, asked before ads load and changeable at any time. Without consent, Google shows non-personalized ads.

3. How long we keep it

  • Information on your device: deleted immediately when you use 'Delete everything' in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Server account and records (if you signed in): kept until you delete the account, then deleted immediately (removal from Google's backups may take up to several dozen days).
  • Ad-related information is kept by Google under Google's policies.

4. Ads

The test is free and the app is supported by Google AdMob ads.

  • Rewarded ad: once when you start a test. Watch it to the end to open the test.
  • Banner ad: above the bottom menu.
  • App open ad: when you move between screens, at most once every 3 minutes.

Ad content is limited to general and parental guidance (PG) ratings. Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent to personalized ads first and can change it anytime in More → Ad privacy settings.

To limit personalized ads, on Android go to Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID; on iOS go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. This app does not ask for tracking permission on iOS. How Google uses information from apps that use its services is explained at Google's partner sites page.

6. Children's privacy

This app is for parents and other adult caregivers. It is not directed to children, and children should not use it themselves. Information about a child is entered by the caregiver, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children (including children under 13 under the US COPPA). When sharing results, we recommend using a nickname instead of your child's real name. If you believe a child has given us information directly, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights and deletion

  • Information on your device: delete it yourself with 'Delete everything' in the app or by uninstalling. Links you have already shared must be deleted by you.
  • Account and server records: delete them in the app (Home → Account → Delete account) or without the app on the account deletion page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws such as California's CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Contact us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.
  • EEA and UK users may also complain to their local data protection authority.
  • We do not sell personal information. Personalized AdMob ads may count as 'sharing' for targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out using your device's ad settings in section 4.

8. Security

  • All transmission is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • If you don't sign in, child information and test records are not sent to a server, except for the anonymous statistics above.
  • If you sign in, security rules let only your own verified account read and write your server records.
